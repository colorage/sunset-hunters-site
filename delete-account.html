Delete your Sunset Hunters Club account

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account. Confirm the request to delete your account and associated photos.

If you cannot access the app, email hi@siaroza.com from your account email address and ask for account deletion. We may need to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

We delete the account and associated photos, likes, and profile data immediately upon in-app deletion (with background storage cleanup running hourly), or within 30 days for verified email requests. Minimal moderation and security records may be retained where required to enforce account restrictions or comply with legal obligations.
