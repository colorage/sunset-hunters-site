Sunset Hunters Club Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026
Controller: Sergey Pekhteerau, Poland · Privacy contact: hi@siaroza.com

Sunset Hunters Club lets account holders photograph sunsets and share them with other users. This policy explains the data used by the Android app and its Supabase backend.

Data we use

  • Account: email address, nickname, authentication identifiers, and password hash managed by Supabase Auth. Google or Apple sign-in provides an identity token when selected.
  • Public posts: sunset photo, thumbnail, nickname, capture time, precise capture coordinates, city/country label, and likes. Other app users can see posts on the feed and map. Public image URLs can be shared outside the app.
  • Safety: reports, blocks, rule acceptance, image hashes, moderation outcomes, and review notes. New photos and thumbnails are sent to OpenAI's image moderation API before publication. Rejected uploads are removed from staging, subject to cleanup retries.
  • Device: foreground location to calculate the sunset window and label photos; camera to capture a photo; optional local notification permission for sunset reminders. The app stores session and preference data on the device.
  • Support: information you send when requesting help or account deletion.

The app has no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not register remote push tokens.

Why we use it

We process account and post data to perform our service contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); foreground location, camera, and optional local notifications with your device permission/consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 6(1)(b)); and safety/moderation data to keep the community safe and comply with platform and legal obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(c)).

Who receives it

Supabase (EU Central / Frankfurt region) hosts authentication, database, storage, and Edge Functions. OpenAI processes newly uploaded images for automated safety moderation. Google or Apple processes sign-in when you choose that method. Map tiles and external geocoding/map resources receive standard network metadata when the map loads. International transfers outside the EEA are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable.

Retention and deletion

Posts remain until you delete them or your account, or they are removed after moderation. Unfinished staged uploads are scheduled for cleanup after 24 hours. Account deletion in Profile → Settings → Delete account removes the account, associated posts, likes, profile data, and stored files (with asynchronous storage cleanup retried hourly). Email deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You can also view deletion instructions at our account deletion page.

Your choices and rights

You can deny camera, location, or notifications through device settings; capture or reminders may then be unavailable. You can delete your own photo, block another user, export a machine-readable JSON copy of your data (Profile → Settings → Export my data), and request account deletion in Settings. Under GDPR, you may also request access, correction, portability, restriction, or erasure, or object to processing by contacting hi@siaroza.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (UODO) or your local supervisory authority.

Children

Sunset Hunters Club is intended for users aged 16 and older (or the minimum digital consent age in your country) and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

We will update the date and policy when processing changes. Contact hi@siaroza.com about privacy or safety concerns.
